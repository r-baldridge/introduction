## What a fraction means

A fraction describes part of a whole. The bottom number, the denominator, says how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The top number, the numerator, says how many of those parts you have.

In 3/4, the whole is cut into 4 equal parts and you have 3 of them.

## Equivalent fractions

Different fractions can name the same amount. Multiplying or dividing the top and bottom by the same number does not change the amount.

- 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8
- 6/9 = 2/3 (divide top and bottom by 3)

Dividing until no common factor is left is called simplifying.

## Comparing fractions

If two fractions have the same denominator, the one with the bigger numerator is bigger. If the denominators differ, rewrite both with a common denominator first.

To compare 2/3 and 3/5, rewrite both in fifteenths: 10/15 and 9/15. So 2/3 is bigger.

Q: In the fraction 5/8, what does the 8 tell you?
A: The whole is split into 8 equal parts (the denominator).

Q: In the fraction 5/8, what does the 5 tell you?
A: You have 5 of those parts (the numerator).

Q: Simplify 6/9.
A: 2/3 — divide the top and bottom by 3.

Q: Name a fraction equivalent to 1/2.
A: Any n/2n, for example 2/4, 3/6, or 4/8.
